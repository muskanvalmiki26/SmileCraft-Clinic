Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit. Can't find an answer? Call us anytime.

Is the root canal treatment painful?
+
Modern root canal treatment is virtually painless. We use advanced local anesthesia techniques, and for anxious patients, we offer conscious sedation. Most patients report that the procedure feels no more uncomfortable than a routine filling. Post-procedure soreness is mild and managed with over-the-counter pain relief.
How long do dental implants last?
+
With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium implant itself is a permanent fixture — only the crown on top may need replacement after 10–15 years due to normal wear. Our implant success rate at SmileCraft is over 99%, backed by a 5-year structural warranty.
How many sessions does teeth whitening take?
+
Our in-chair laser whitening delivers results in a single 60–90 minute session — typically 6 to 8 shades brighter. For more gradual results, we offer custom take-home whitening trays used over 10–14 days. We'll recommend the best option based on your tooth sensitivity and desired outcome during a free consultation.
What is the difference between braces and clear aligners?
+
Traditional metal or ceramic braces are fixed and ideal for more complex orthodontic cases. Clear aligners are removable, nearly invisible, and more comfortable — great for mild to moderate misalignment in adults who want a discreet option. Both deliver excellent results; your orthodontist will assess which is right for your specific case at your consultation.
Do you handle dental emergencies?
+
Yes — we have dedicated emergency slots every day, including weekends. If you're experiencing a severe toothache, broken tooth, knocked-out tooth, or dental abscess, call us immediately or WhatsApp us. We aim to see emergency patients within the same day. After hours, you can reach our on-call dentist via our emergency WhatsApp line.
Does SmileCraft accept dental insurance?
+
We accept most major corporate dental insurance plans and cashless treatment through empanelled insurers. Our patient care team will verify your coverage before your appointment and handle all paperwork. We also offer easy no-cost EMI options through major banks and NBFCs for treatments not covered by insurance.
How often should I visit the dentist?
+
We recommend a professional dental checkup and cleaning every 6 months for most adults. Patients with gum disease, braces, or a higher cavity risk may benefit from more frequent visits — typically every 3 to 4 months. Children should have their first dental visit by age 1 or when their first tooth appears, and then every 6 months thereafter.
